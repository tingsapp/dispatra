# Dispatra Web Client Specification

> Normative React/TypeScript dispatcher and organization web application contract. `MUST`, `MUST NOT`, `SHOULD`, and `MAY` are intentional.
> Last updated: 2026-09-20

## 1. Purpose and authority

The web client serves organization admins, dispatchers, and the limited customer-facing tracking projection. It provides Monitor, Orders, Routes, Drivers, Vehicles, Shippers, Analytics, Organization Settings, Profile, Help, and invoice views. The FastAPI/PostgreSQL API owns authorization, state transitions, pricing, dispatch, route feasibility, POD acceptance, and invoice truth. The client MUST NOT invent or bypass those rules.

Dispatra V1 is a simple English-only local-delivery system for British Columbia, Canada. The fixed stack is React, TypeScript strict mode, Vite, shadcn/ui, and `react-map-gl` with `maplibre-gl`. The UI follows the light ChatGPT-style application shell: a #fcfcfc 260px sidebar with a subtle 1px right border, #fcfcfc body and page canvas, white control and floating surfaces, neutral grey hover/selected states, a borderless page heading, Inter/system sans-serif typography, black pill-shaped primary actions, rounded fields and restrained overlays. The shared theme in `src/styles/theme.css` supplies neutral tokens to both the operational prototype and authenticated portals. Semantic status colors and accessible non-map alternatives remain intact.

The client must support both final API-connected behavior and the current static/local-data phase. Static domain models MUST mirror intended API schemas so the transition does not rewrite the UI.

Authenticated workspaces share the sidebar, page header and control styling through a presentational shell without importing operational prototype stores. On narrow screens, sidebars open over the content, trap keyboard focus while open, dismiss with Escape or the backdrop, and restore focus to their trigger. Toggling navigation must preserve mounted form drafts.

The collapsed logo expands the sidebar without navigating; do not show a separate expand icon. In the expanded sidebar, the brand retains its workspace home action, and the collapse icon stays neutral muted gray in both resting and hover states. Hide text labels visually, retain accessible names, and show hover titles for icon actions. Active destination icons have no background in the collapsed rail; expanded navigation retains its selected background. The operational rail also retains the account avatar and Auto dispatch switch. ## 2. Shared domain contract

Use Order as the commercial/pricing/invoice unit. An optional DeliveryJob is an operational child, never a competing lifecycle. A Route belongs to one driver and one vehicle and may contain multiple Orders. A RouteStop is a stable-ID pickup or drop-off visit; repeated addresses remain separate. V1 supports all pickup/drop-off cardinalities and interleaved sequences, provided linked pickup precedes delivery and capacity is valid at each leg.

Order lifecycle is `NEW -> ASSIGNED -> IN_PROGRESS -> COMPLETED -> INVOICED`, with `CANCELLED` as the only other status (user decision, September 22). Exceptions are attention flags layered on top of the status, never statuses: Pricing needs review (price could not be produced), At risk, Late start and Failed attempt; each clears when its cause goes away, feeds the Monitor's Needs Attention list and the Orders "Needs attention" filter, and is shown as a note beside the status. Completed orders offer an Invoice action until invoiced. Retired values (`DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED`, `PRICED`, `READY_FOR_DISPATCH`, `NEEDS_ATTENTION` → NEW; `IN_EXECUTION` → IN_PROGRESS; `BILLING_FINALIZATION` → COMPLETED; `FAILED` → CANCELLED) are normalised when older saved orders load. Do not create dozens of client-only statuses. Route, stop, assignment, pricing, and invoice statuses are separate.

Exactly two organization dispatch modes exist: `AUTO` and `MANUAL`. AUTO assigns feasible READY Orders and automatically optimizes. MANUAL leaves READY Orders unassigned until a dispatcher chooses Driver/Vehicle, then the backend validates and automatically optimizes. AI may recommend in Manual but MUST NOT assign. Neither mode may rewrite locked/in-progress routes automatically. Drivers cannot self-assign. Maximum Active Orders per Driver is edited in driver details and saved on that driver as `maxActiveOrders`. Existing drivers without a saved value and new driver forms use the retained company `max_active_orders_per_driver` default. The limit must be a whole number of at least 1, applies to both local Manual and Auto/recommendation assignment paths, and never changes existing assignments or customer prices.

The client displays customer price from API PricingSnapshot/ChargeLines and may use the same centralized local pricing module for static scenarios. Customer price is independent of driver, deadhead, workload, merged route distance, dispatcher choice, and later optimization. Show priced order distance separately from operational route distance and deadhead. Invoice lines come from finalized ChargeLines; normal completion generates an idempotent Invoice and sends it to the customer's billing email.

## 3. Static-domain architecture

Until API integration, centralize local data and behavior in a small domain layer, conceptually:

```text
src/domain/       models, statuses, validation
src/mock/         centralized static store and scenarios
src/pricing/      rate resolution and calculatePricing
src/dispatch/     eligibility and evaluateAssignment
src/routing/      optimizeRoute simulation and validation
src/billing/      finalized pricing and invoice simulation
```

Required shared functions resemble `resolveRateCard(order, context)`, `calculatePricing(order, context)`, `evaluateAssignment(order, drivers, vehicles, routes, settings)`, and `optimizeRoute(routeInput)`. Pages and visual components consume results; they MUST NOT duplicate formulas, state machines, or dispatch decisions. Static scenarios MUST include:

1. Base + Distance order; 2. Direct multi-stop; 3. customer-specific Zone; 4. Fixed independent of dimensional weight; 5. Hourly actual-duration settlement; 6. Imported price; 7. zone no-match Needs Attention; 8. Rate Card conflict Needs Attention; 9. missing distance price unavailable; 10. driver at maximum ineligible in AUTO; 11. multi-pickup/multi-drop optimized Route; 12. intermediate capacity overload; 13. completion -> finalized price -> Invoice -> Sent; 14. invoice send failure; 15. locked price unchanged after reassignment; 16. operational distance changes while customer price stays unchanged.

Demo monetary values MUST be labelled static/demo values, not industry defaults.

## 4. Monitor

The first Monitor visit in a tab session plays a short space-to-Vancouver introduction: a satellite globe initially showing the Asia/Australia side against a star field, an immediate fast zoom while rotating horizontally east across the Pacific toward Canada, an eight-second descent that progressively slows into Vancouver, a 350ms settling pause, and a 700ms fade into the normal operational camera. The sequence takes about ten and a half seconds from local style initialization, without an opening hold or waiting for remote imagery. A bundled low-resolution satellite tile provides the initial Earth surface while detailed imagery loads. Keep latitude fixed at Vancouver’s latitude and bearing/pitch north-up throughout the flight so Earth never tilts or rotates vertically. Rotation slows to a stop over Canada; the Vancouver label and destination dot appear as Canada turns into view. Render this on a separate temporary map while the existing map, routes and data initialize underneath. The animation must not change the operational map's camera, style, selections or controller. Reveal normally after the flight and the underlying map's load event; always release the workspace within thirteen seconds if loading stalls. Provide Skip intro and Escape dismissal, bypass reduced-motion users and locate-on-map entries, and never replay on ordinary return visits or refresh within the same tab session. Navigation away, backgrounding, imagery failure and intro WebGL failure dispose of the temporary scene. Keep mobile navigation reachable during playback and restore focus after keyboard dismissal. The existing satellite provider supplies the intro imagery with visible attribution.

Monitor is the default operational screen. The sidebar logo and Dispatra wordmark form one keyboard-accessible control that returns to Monitor through the existing guarded navigation. There is no separate Monitor item in the sidebar list. It uses a full-height Vancouver, British Columbia map rendered with `react-map-gl` and `maplibre-gl`. There is no separate permanent top header over the map. Keep one compact 260px sidebar and active routes visible. Both show/hide controls use the same thin outlined panel icon. Enabled links, buttons and other interactive controls use `cursor: pointer` throughout the client; text inputs and the draggable map retain their appropriate cursors. Floating top-right controls are search, notifications, and date, on white backgrounds. Bottom-right controls are map settings and zoom; Map/Satellite/Traffic/Labels options stay hidden until the settings menu opens. Use contextual popovers/dialogs close to the source. Keep account/profile in one account menu. Notifications are accessed only from the Monitor control over the map, with no duplicate Notifications item in the account menu. The account card has a subtle neutral background with no border at rest. Open and keyboard-focus states use the same neutral grey highlights as sidebar items; only keyboard focus adds an inset focus ring. The menu sits above it with a clear gap. Clicking outside dismisses the menu; Escape dismisses it and returns focus to the account card.

Show concise Active Routes/Orders, Available Drivers, and Needs Attention surfaces. Driver and Order markers have semantic tooltips/popovers; selected route/driver detail is on demand. Needs Attention rows state what happened, why it matters, and the recommended action. Examples include pricing unavailable/conflict, no eligible driver, maximum count, infeasible/capacity/precedence route, stale location, failed pickup, missing POD, missing billing data, and invoice email failure.

Expose a sidebar-footer switch with the single label `Auto dispatch` (on = AUTO, off = MANUAL); visually distinguish the active state, confirm changes, and show feedback. Static behavior is AUTO ready Order -> simulated assignment -> optimized Route, or MANUAL ready Order -> unassigned -> dispatcher Assign Driver -> optimized Route. API behavior is authoritative after integration.

Non-Monitor operational pages use the shared PageHeader: a borderless page heading with a clear title, muted description and optional page-specific actions. Do not show a Back to Monitor button in page headers; the Dispatra logo and wordmark provide that navigation. Content retains a readable maximum width for operational tables and forms. When navigation is hidden, the show-sidebar control occupies the left of the title bar without covering its title. Desktop and mobile headers wrap actions as needed. Drivers are one of two kinds — Employee (drives a company vehicle) or Owner-operator (drives their own vehicle, which is still a Vehicles record) — chosen on a flat driver form: name, number, phone, email, Account, Duty, Employment, Attached vehicle, Maximum Active Orders per Driver and service areas. Work status, shift start/end, licence, qualifications, work limits, depots and availability schedules are not on the form. Employment never affects customer price. Drivers and Vehicles use tables with visible, keyboard-accessible Details actions opening the full record panel. Company tabs have no divider beneath the tab row; save availability indicates pending edits without an always-visible unsaved label.

## 5. Organization Settings and pricing

The September 12 user-approved rules in [pricing-rules.md](pricing-rules.md) govern calculation order and explicit contract controls. Base + Distance never charges routine time; hourly contracts explicitly define their clock and inclusions. Minimum freight is applied after the service charge, and minimum order subtotal after discounts/manual adjustments, both excluding tax. Fuel uses eligible lines only. Discount inheritance is Customer → Rate Card, where INHERIT continues and NONE stops. Zone prices may be contractual and use explicit pickup-to-delivery movements. Imported final agreed totals are preserved with explicit tax treatment. Margin is an informational estimate, not a price adjustment. There is no margin target, target comparison, warning or target-based display colour. Operating Costs contains only cost inputs; Order views may show estimated cost, profit and actual estimated margin. Existing quoted terms and finalized amounts remain frozen.
