# Dispatra web client specification

Updated: 2026-10-15. This is the current React/TypeScript client contract. [Implementation state](state.md) distinguishes browser demo behavior from API-backed behavior.

Input focus uses a thin black border across native inputs, textareas, selects, grouped inputs, editable table cells, custom select/date triggers and chat composers. Field focus keeps the existing rounded border shape; do not draw an overlapping inset outline over that border. Keep validation error colours and system high-contrast focus indicators.

Weight and vehicle-dimension form fields display at most two decimal places after unit conversion, trimming unnecessary trailing zeroes. Opening or saving an unchanged form preserves canonical stored measurements; do not round database values for display.

## Public entity IDs

Orders, Drivers and Shippers have server-issued public numbers in the format `D{company initial}{entity letter}-{random number}`. Entity letters are O, D and S respectively. The company initial is the first Latin letter in the company name, falling back to the slug. Random numbers start with four digits and expand only after that company/entity exhausts its combinations. Generation is serialized per company and checked against archived and active numbers; database uniqueness remains enforced. These published numbers are stable across profile edits and company renames.

Vehicles use `D{company initial}V-{unit number}`, such as `DDV-V12`. Unit numbers retain letters and leading zeros, are trimmed and uppercased, and must be unique within the company, including archived vehicles. Editing the unit number updates the public ID suffix; the issued company prefix survives company renames. UUIDs remain internal relation keys. Migration updates existing vehicle IDs without changing UUIDs, assignments, or saved commercial history; duplicate or invalid legacy units must be corrected before migration. Frozen commercial snapshots retain their saved historical text.
